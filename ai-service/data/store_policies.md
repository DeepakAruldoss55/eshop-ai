# DeepShop Store Knowledge & Policies

## About DeepShop
DeepShop is a curated modern tech and gear e-commerce boutique specializing in next-generation studio acoustics, high-performance computing, curved OLED displays, smart wearables, and premium creator ergonomics.

## Shipping & Delivery Policy
- **Free Priority Shipping**: Free carbon-neutral priority shipping is automatically applied to all orders over $200.
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: Flat rate of $9.99 for orders under $200.
- **Delivery Timeframes**:
  - Domestic express: 2-3 business days.
  - Standard ground: 3-5 business days.
  - Same-day dispatch: Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST ship the same day from our fulfillment centers.
- **Carriers**: BlueDart Express, FedEx Priority Global, and Standard Ground Express.
- **Real-Time GPS Order Tracking**: Every order includes an end-to-end tracking code (e.g., `TRK-DPS-4589741`). Customers can track live checkpoints in the "Track Orders" modal.

## 2-Year Manufacturer Warranty Guarantee
- Every electronic device, headphone, smartwatch, and monitor purchased on DeepShop comes with an automatic **2-Year Comprehensive Manufacturer Warranty**.
- Coverage includes hardware defects, mechanical failure, driver malfunction, internal battery degradation under 80%, and firmware issues.
- Fast warranty claim process: If a product cannot be repaired, we issue a brand-new replacement within 48 hours.

## 30-Day Hassle-Free Returns & Refunds
- **Return Window**: Customers have 30 calendar days from the date of delivery to initiate a return.
- **Condition**: Items must be in original condition with packaging and included accessories.
- **Return Shipping**: Pre-paid return shipping labels are provided at zero cost to the customer for defective or unsatisfied items.
- **Refund Speed**: Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 3-5 business days upon receipt and inspection at our logistics hub.

## Payment & Checkout Security
- **Supported Payment Methods**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and encrypted one-click checkout.
- **Security**: 256-bit SSL encryption, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance. We never store raw card details.
